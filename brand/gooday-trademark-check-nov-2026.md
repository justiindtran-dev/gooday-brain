# Gooday trade mark check (22 Nov 2026)

**Not legal advice.** This is a desk search to flag risk before you talk to a trade mark attorney.

Brand names are protected by **trade marks**, not patents. So this checks trade marks.

## Verdict: risky but not blocked in Australia. More risky in the US.

I found no live Australian mark for the exact word GOODAY on food or supplements. There are nearby "G'DAY" and "GOOD DAY" marks in the food classes, though. In the US, a GOODDAY mark covering supplements and drinks has already been filed.

**Limit:** this cloud session couldn't reach IP Australia's live search tool directly. I worked from register pages that search engines had indexed. Anything filed recently or not indexed would not show up. Run the free check below before you file.

## Brands, domains and handles

- **gooday.com.au is taken.** It belongs to an AI booking and personal-assistant app called Gooday, apparently Sydney-based. That's a different industry, so it's no trade mark problem for food, but you can't use that domain. A company called **Gooday Australia Group Pty Ltd** is also registered.
- I didn't find a Gooday food or supplement brand selling in Australia.
- I couldn't check Instagram handle availability from here. Check @gooday, @goodayau and @gooday.co by hand.

## Recommended next steps

1. **Free check:** run "Gooday" through IP Australia's TM Checker and Australian Trade Mark Search (search.ipaustralia.gov.au) in classes 5, 29, 30 and 32.
2. **Best value: TM Headstart, $200 per class.** IP Australia gives you a preliminary view on whether the G'DAY mark blocks you, and you can then convert it into a full application for $130. With two classes the minimum is about $660.
3. **Classes to file:** 5 (supplements, collagen and fibre products) and probably 29 (jellies). Add 30 if you want confectionery cover. With the picklist, a standard filing costs $250 per class, so two to three classes is **about $500 to $750 in government fees**. Registration takes at least 7 months.
4. **Get an attorney's opinion** on the G'DAY / "Gooday" similarity before printing pouches at scale. My guess is a few hundred to about $1,500 for a clearance opinion. No source here gave a price.
5. **For the US:** watch GOODDAY 98748005. If it registers, "Gooday" in supplements would be hard in the US. You might need a modifier (for example "Gooday Jelly") or a different US name.
