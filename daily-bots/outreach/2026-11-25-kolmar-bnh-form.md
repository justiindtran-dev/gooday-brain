Hello Kolmar BNH team,

I'm Justin, founder of Gooday. I read that Kolmar BNH makes the Boots UK K-wellness range, including the fibre jelly and collagen jelly sticks, and I'd like to ask about OEM/ODM production for our brand.

About us: Gooday is a new Australian brand (not on sale yet). We plan to sell single-serve squeezable jelly as a general food in Australia, and we're looking for an OEM/ODM partner.

The two products:

1. Fibre jelly
- About 7 g dietary fibre per serve, from resistant dextrin and acacia (gum arabic). A little partially hydrolysed guar gum (PHGG) is welcome if you have it.
- About 1 to 1.5 g greens powder per serve (spinach, kale, broccoli, spirulina).
- Gel: apple pectin (low-methoxyl) or gellan.

2. Collagen jelly
- About 6 g hydrolysed collagen peptides per serve. We haven't chosen bovine or marine (fish) collagen yet, so please quote both.
- Optional: a small amount of Kakadu plum powder (a natural fruit powder).
- Gel: gellan or pectin. Gelatin is OK as a fallback.

Both products:
- Flavours: apple, peach, grape.
- Under 2 g sugar per serve, sweetened with stevia (Reb M) and monk fruit. No erythritol, sucralose or polyols.
- No added vitamins or minerals.
- No konjac and no glucomannan. These are not allowed in jelly in Australia, and we need this in writing. No carrageenan unless we agree first.
- The jelly must stay soft enough to drink through a spout.
- Shelf stable for 12 months at room temperature.
- Formats: a spout pouch of about 120 to 150 g, and/or a tear-and-squeeze stick of 20 to 30 g. Please quote both if you can.

Could you please tell me:
1. Can you make these two jellies (especially 7 g fibre in a konjac-free gel)? Have you made something similar?
2. Sample cost, and the cost to ship samples to Melbourne, Australia.
3. MOQ per flavour/SKU, and the MOQ for printed pouches or film.
4. Unit price at your MOQ and at two or three higher tiers (for example 30,000 / 50,000 / 100,000 units), ex-works and FOB.
5. Lead times for samples and for the first production run.
6. Can you source all the ingredients, or should we supply some (for example the collagen or the Kakadu plum)?
7. Your certifications (HACCP, ISO 22000 or FSSC 22000, GMP, halal). Copies would be great.
8. Your experience exporting to Australia: FSANZ labelling, imported food inspection, and import paperwork for collagen or gelatin.
9. Pouch and stick options, and private-label printing.
10. Whether you can run a texture and stability (syneresis) test on the sample.

Thank you, and I look forward to hearing from you.
