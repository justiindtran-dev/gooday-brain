Hello ATIQ Food team,

I'm Justin, founder of Gooday. I found your private-label jelly page and noticed you offer squeeze sachets and 130-150 g spout pouches, with pectin and alginate gels in your functional range.

About us: Gooday is a new Australian brand (not on sale yet). We plan to sell single-serve squeezable jelly as a general food in Australia, and we're looking for an OEM/ODM partner.

The two products:

1. Fibre jelly
- About 7 g dietary fibre per serve, from resistant dextrin and acacia (gum arabic). A little partially hydrolysed guar gum (PHGG) is welcome if you have it.
- About 1 to 1.5 g greens powder per serve (spinach, kale, broccoli, spirulina).
- Gel: apple pectin (low-methoxyl) or gellan.

2. Collagen jelly
- About 6 g hydrolysed collagen peptides per serve. We haven't chosen bovine or marine (fish) collagen yet, so please quote both.
