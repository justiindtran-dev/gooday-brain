4 approved ads vs the rejected apple Number stack (25 Nov). Bot's reading; Justin hasn't said why.

## Style guide (1080x1350)
- **Colour:** one saturated solid field matched to the flavour (peach, deep green, maroon, teal). Type in cream or white, plus one dark ink. *Rejected:* a pale mint gradient with low contrast.
- **Type size:** a 2-line headline at about 100 to 150 px a line. Biggest element, even weight. *Rejected:* giant numbers next to tiny unit words.
- **Layout:** headline in the top third. Big, sharp pouch (40 to 55% of the height), centred or on the right. 2 to 3 chips and a dark pill CTA. *Rejected:* ruled table rows, a small cropped pouch and an empty lower third.
- **Copy:** a 2 to 5 word headline with full stops, a subline of 8 words or fewer, one idea. *Rejected:* three competing facts and a calorie lead (diet-coded).
