# Nutritionist bot: formula benchmark and recommendation (23 Nov 2026)

Research only. Nobody was contacted. Not medical advice. This is a proposal, and Justin decides. Full page with tables, doses and links: [brain/formulation.md](../../brain/formulation.md). The open decision is in [brain/decisions.md](../../brain/decisions.md).

## What I did
- Benchmarked 18 products per serve (9 fibre/greens, 9 collagen), with links checked 23 Nov 2026.
- Built an effective-dose table for 14 candidate ingredients (RCT and meta-analysis doses, GI limits).
- Wrote exact formulas for both SKUs in the 30 g stick and the 120 g pouch, with good / better / best options.

## Recommended (the "better" option)
- **Fibre + greens, green apple:**
  - Actives: resistant dextrin 4.5 g + acacia 2.5 g + PHGG 1.0 g, set with amidated LM pectin (1.3% w/w in the stick, 0.6% in the pouch) and calcium. Greens are 1.0 g in the stick and 1.5 g in the pouch (spinach, kale, broccoli, spirulina; no wheatgrass).
  - Sweetener and acid: Reb M + monk fruit, malic and citric acid, pH 3.5.
  - Per serve: **7 g fibre on the label** (7.7 g designed), about 21 kcal (stick) or 26 kcal (pouch), under 1 g sugar (stick) or under 2 g (pouch).
  - Claims: "excellent source of fibre", "fibre contributes to regular laxation".
  - Ingredient cost: A$0.15 to 0.17 EST.
- **Collagen + Kakadu plum, peach:**
  - Actives: 6.0 g bovine collagen peptides (about 5.4 g protein), Kakadu plum 0.25 g + acerola 0.15 g (about 46 mg vitamin C at fill; claim 10 mg until stability data).
  - Gel: gellan HA/LA at 0.2% (stick) or 0.11% (pouch). Gelatin is the fallback.
  - Per serve: about 27 kcal (stick) or 32 kcal (pouch), under 1 g sugar (stick) or under 2 g (pouch).
  - Claims: "source of protein", "good source of vitamin C", and vitamin C "contributes to normal collagen formation for the normal function of skin" (Schedule 4).
  - Ingredient cost: A$0.22 to 0.24 EST.
- **Both SKUs:** no konjac, carrageenan, erythritol, sucralose, polyols or added vitamins.

## Biggest differences vs competitors
1. **7 g of gentle fibre with no inulin or chicory.** Grüns, Metamucil and Physician's Choice use 5 to 6 g, mostly inulin. Only Jelly Me hits 7 g, and it uses a konjac gel.
2. **6 g of real collagen in a stick.** Jelly Me has 2 g, FANCL 3 g, Earth Pharmaceutical about 2.5 g, and Everydaze uses "hibiscus collagen". Ours is enough for a protein claim, and its food-derived vitamin C unlocks the only permitted skin-related claim.
3. **Clean sweetening and no added vitamins.** Competitors use erythritol, maltitol, sucralose, ace-K or xylitol, and AG1, Bloom and Grüns add vitamins. Gooday uses stevia + monk fruit and fits the AU general-food route.

## Needs before the formula is final
- **Compliance and the consultant:** check the claims and the NPSC. Decide whether Kakadu overage counts as an "added vitamin", and which food class the product falls in.
- **Factory:** gel and syneresis trial, hot-fill validation.
- **AU lab:** fibre by AOAC 2009.01/2011.25, protein, and vitamin C stability.
- **Finance:** re-run COGS at these ingredient costs.
- **Biggest risk:** a no-konjac gel at about 28% solids, and collagen–pectin/gellan haze.
