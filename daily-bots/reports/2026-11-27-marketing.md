**Method (Justin's realism feedback, run-2231 method):** every design uses real photos from Wikimedia Commons, downscaled with no upscaling except D3 (1.05x, see below). The round 3 v3 pack is re-rendered flat and built as opaque printed film: pillowed body, crimped heat seals, light creases, one satin highlight from the photo's key light. It is then matched to the photo for levels, colour temperature, bounce light and softness. Each pack has a contact shadow, a cast shadow projected onto the ground plane, and a faint reflection on semi-gloss surfaces. One grain pass and one JPEG pass cover the whole image. There are no fake droplets and no glare streaks. The claim panel is soft-focused. **New this run:** the pouch stands upright at eye level, in place of last night's overhead fruit piles, so no concept repeats.
