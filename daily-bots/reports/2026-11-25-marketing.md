## 1. D1_B_NumberStack.png (Angle B, fibre, green apple)
- **Copy:** "6 g fibre." / "about 25 kcal." / "0 scoops." (three ruled rows) / "One squeeze-and-go pouch." / CTA "Join the waitlist".
- **Inspired by:** DAVID's 203-day three-number static. It keeps the stack of three facts with full stops, then swaps protein, calories and sugar for fibre, kcal and "0 scoops". The third number is the format win, not a nutrient.
- **v1: 3/4/3/4/3/3/5.** Flaws: the three lines are a wall of equal text with no hierarchy; the pouch is small and pushed into the corner, leaving the bottom third empty; the pale background is flat.
- **v2: 4/4/4/5/4/4/5.** Changes: the numbers became giant and the units small, and the pouch is bigger. Flaws: "~25" reads as a hedge and isn't the Green wording ("About 25 kcal"); the numbers in the green accent lack contrast; the rows float with no structure.
- **Final (v3): 5/5/5/5/4/4/5.** Changes: ruled rows in a "nutrition label/receipt" style, the numbers in dark ink, "about" restored next to 25, a white glow behind the pouch, and a "One squeeze-and-go pouch." line to fill the gap.
- **Note:** it is number-led like 24 Nov D3 Big Six, which Justin didn't pick. The difference is the three-fact rhythm (and the "0 scoops" twist) versus one giant number. If Justin dislikes number-led ads, drop this one first.
