## 3 patterns worth borrowing
1. **"[Thing] isn't [thing] without…" seasonal pun** (74-day Grüns caption). We keep the sentence shape and drop the character and the discount. Grüns' season is a US winter. Ours is Aussie summer, which no one in the swipes is using.
2. **Speech bubbles over a real hand-held pack** (best Grüns rank, 4%). It looks native to the feed and casual, not like an ad. Our version uses the founder's own true words, never a made-up customer.
3. **One oversized product as the whole ad** (giant gummy, 7% and 17% ranks). One object fills the frame on a solid field with very little copy. This fits Justin's taste: a big pouch on a saturated field.

## 2 patterns to avoid
1. **Discount and scarcity theatre**: "Up to 55% off", "Limited edition", "Steal holiday flavor drop", "Over 100,000 reviews" badges, plus licensed characters. We are not on sale. Fake urgency or social proof is an ACCC risk and Red in claims.md. Licensed IP is off-limits.
2. **Life-stage benefit diagrams** ("[Life stage] support, simplified." with arrows to nutrient benefits). Each arrow is a health claim (Amber/Red for us). Naming a life stage implies knowledge of the viewer's body, which Meta's rules forbid ("for people who…", not "you have…").
