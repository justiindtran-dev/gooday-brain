## Friday 20 Nov, not in yesterday's report (older than the window, kept for context)

3. **20 Nov: The peptide boom brings challenges and opportunities for supplements (New Hope)**
   Unapproved peptide drugs (BPC-157, TB-500) are being sold as supplements. That puts the reputation of real food-derived peptides, such as collagen peptides, at risk.
   https://www.newhope.com/natural-product-trends/the-peptide-boom-births-challenges-and-opportunities-for-supplement-industry
   *Why it matters for Gooday:* The Peach collagen pouch should say "collagen peptides (food-derived)" clearly and never borrow the "peptide" hype language.

4. **20 Nov: Sixth Street pays US$350M for a minority stake in Chomps (New Hope)**
   This is one of the biggest better-for-you snack deals this year. The founders stay majority owners. No valuation was disclosed.
   https://www.newhope.com/investments/private-equity-sixth-street-invests-350-million-minority-stake-chomps
   *Why it matters for Gooday:* Investors are still paying for single-serve, gym-bag, high-protein or functional snacks. That supports pitching Gooday as a "snack format" rather than a "supplement".

5. **20 Nov: Tonum Health and Euvita telehealth partner on GLP-1 protein support (Nutrition Insight)**
   US telehealth is now bundling supplements and 24 g protein bars into physician-led GLP-1 programmes.
   https://www.nutritioninsight.com/news/tonum-health-euvita-telehealth-supplements-glp1-protein.html
   *Why it matters for Gooday:* GLP-1 companion products are moving into the clinic channel in the US. In AU, telehealth GLP-1 providers could be a later partner channel, but Gooday's own ads must still never mention the drugs.

6. **20 Nov: WHO, UNICEF and 21 countries launch "Bending the curve by 2030" on obesity (Nutrition Insight)**
   The plan backs sugar-drink taxes and rules on ultra-processed food. The food industry body IFBA calls it misguided.
   https://www.nutritioninsight.com/news/who-unicef-obesity-call-to-action-2030.html
   *Why it matters for Gooday:* Sugar and UPF rules are tightening. Gooday's ~25 kcal, low-sugar pouch fits that direction, so keep sugar per serve front and centre on pack.
