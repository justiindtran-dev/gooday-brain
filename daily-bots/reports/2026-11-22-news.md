   https://www.nutritionaloutlook.com/view/positive-aging-glp-1-support--gut-health-kemins-top-trends

3. **20 Nov: NutraCast: Akkermansia research points to postbiotic potential for metabolic health and weight maintenance**
   Prof. Willem de Vos says pasteurised Akkermansia may work as well as live cells. He sees a postbiotic role in preventing weight regain after GLP-1 drugs are stopped.
   https://nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/11/20/nutracast-akkermansia-research-points-to-postbiotic-potential-for-metabolic-health-weight-maintenance
   *Why it matters for Gooday:* Postbiotics are heat-stable, so they could suit a shelf-stable jelly better than live probiotics. Worth watching for a future fibre and greens SKU (check novel-food/ARTG status in AU first).
