**Method (run-0458 / run-2231 realism method):**
- **Photos:** real CC0 photos from Wikimedia Commons. D1 is downscaled (0.675x). D2 is used at 1:1, under a solid headline band, because its largest available file can't fill 1080x1350 without upscaling. Neither photo is upscaled.
- **Pack:** the round 3 v3 print is built as opaque film with a pillowed body, crimped seals, light creases and one satin highlight. The highlight comes from the photo's own light, which is read from the real shadows: the pencil shadows in D1 and the melon-rind shadows in D2.
- **Grounding:** each pack has an occlusion halo, a cast shadow and a contact line. Grain and the JPEG pass cover the whole image.
- **Not added:** no fake droplets or glare.
- **Claim panel:** soft-focused.
- **New this run:** a real object lies **on top of** the pack. In D1, a pencil from the photo crosses the pack's lower corner and casts its own shadow onto the film.

## D1 · B fibre · "Fibre, between meetings."
- **Scene:** an overhead flat lay on a pale timber desk. A laptop corner, a black coffee, two pencils and a sticky note surround it. The grape fibre pouch lies at an angle, and one pencil rests across its lower corner.
- **Copy:** the headline is in dark green ink on the empty wood. The subline reads "No mixing. No shaker. Just squeeze." The CTA is a dark pill reading "Join the waitlist".
- **Footer:** sits on a 55% dark scrim.
- **Photo:** "Laptop and coffee on a desk.jpg", CC0.

- **v1 flaws:**
  - The pouch read flat, like a sticker, because the pillow shading was weak.
  - The pencil ran under the pack with no sign of contact.
  - The white lower panel was brighter than the real sticky note.
- **v2:** the pencil now lies on top of the pack, which is a real overlap. Pillowing is stronger and the whites are pulled down. New flaw: the pencil covered "6 g fibre".
- **v3:** the pack moved and rotated so the pencil crosses only the soft-focused lower corner, which keeps "6 g fibre" clear. Remaining flaws:
  - The pencil strip looked pale and see-through, because the mask was off-centre.
  - The pack's shadow fell up-left. The photo's own pencil shadows fall to the right, so the light comes from the left.
- **v4:** the pencil mask is re-fitted, so the pencil is solid. Light now comes from the left, the cast shadow falls right, and the pencil's shadow falls onto the film. The maker's imprint on the pencil, a third-party brand, was still faintly readable.
- **Final:** the imprint is softened so it can't be read. **Remaining gaps:** the pack print is cleaner than real film. A pencil lying on a puffed pouch would ride up slightly, and here it stays flat.
