## Key findings (23 Nov 2026)
- **Victoria:** Class 4 (pre-packaged low-risk food) means notification only. Class 3 includes warehousing or distribution of pre-packaged food. A 3PL model is simplest, but ask the council ([health.vic.gov.au](https://health.vic.gov.au/food-safety/food-business-classifications)).
- **Bovine collagen:** beef products are "risk food" (BSE), **but gelatine and collagen from hides and skins are exempt** ([Agriculture](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/import/goods/food/inspection-compliance/risk-food/beef)). Get the factory to confirm the source in writing, and check BICON for a permit.
- **TAFTA:** 0% duty needs a certificate of origin issued by Thailand's Department of Foreign Trade ([ABF guide](https://www.abf.gov.au/free-trade-agreements/files/instructions-guidelines-thailand-aus-fta.pdf)).
- **Subscriptions:** the ACL unfair trading practices law starts **1 Jul 2027**. It requires renewal reminders, easy online cancellation and drip-pricing rules ([McCullough](https://mccullough.com.au/2026/07/03/unfair-trading-practices-laws-australia-2027/)). The drafts already follow it.
- **Privacy:** the tranche 2 exposure draft (Aug 2026) keeps the small business exemption for now ([CBP](https://www.cbp.com.au/insights/publications/privacy-amendment-(personal-data-protection)-bill-2026-exposure-draft-released-for-second-tranche-o)). A policy is still needed for Shopify and Meta.
- **Pre-orders:** take money only once the date is reasonably sure (ACL s36). Until then, use the free waitlist or a refundable deposit.
