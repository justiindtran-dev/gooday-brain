## Method notes (reusable; scripts in `run-2303/source/oldshape/`)
1. **woodfill.py, a clean plate without Poisson seams.** It splits the plate into a low-frequency light level (Telea fill at 1/8 scale) and high-frequency grain. The grain is copied along the measured grain direction (structure tensor). For an eye-level scene with a hand on the left (`WF_SIDE=right`), it tiles the strip of wood to the right of each row's hole, so the depth and blur match.
   - Row shifts (the old method) failed on both photos: they copied the apple, then the stand-in itself, into the hole.
2. **jointfix.py** redraws a straight board joint through the hole by translating a visible joint segment along its own line.
3. **oldshape.py additions:**
   - `sy` foreshortens a pouch lying flat at an angle.
   - `clean_full` takes a precomputed clean plate.
   - `keep_back` keeps the hand pixels behind the pack.
   - `keep_front` restores the real thumb on top, plus a thumb shadow on the film.
   - `contact` adds a contact shadow under an upright pack.
   - `s_blur` softens the shading blotches left by the stand-in's dark artwork.
   - `film_fx:false` turns off misregistration when the print is scaled up by more than 1.3x, to avoid colour fringes.
   - `soften:false` is for the `_g` flats, where the non-Green line is erased instead of blurred.
4. **basefix.py:** under an upright pack, put back the photo's own floor below the base, then add a tight contact shadow.
5. **Shot tips for Justin:**
   - Apple beside the pouch, not behind it.
   - Fingers clearly in front or clearly behind.
   - A green apple (or no fruit) for the apple pack; a peach for collagen.
