## 1. D1_B_FibreYes.png (Angle B, fibre, green grape): "Fibre: yes. Shaker: no."
- **Copy:** "Fibre: yes." (white/dark teal, top half) / "6 g fibre per pouch. Squeeze and go." / "Shaker: no." (grey, bottom half) with a generic crossed-out shaker drawing / CTA "Join the waitlist".
- **Inspired by:** AG1's two-line contrast (4 variants) as a split-screen us-vs-them static. Our "them" is a format, the shaker, in line with the brand voice ("against powders, shakers and grey tubs, never against named brands").
- **v1: 4/4/3/4/4/4/5.** Flaws: the pouch is small and floats beside the split; both headlines are one line at the same size, so the half-and-half reads flat; the grey half has a big dead area and a tiny shaker.
- **v2: 4/5/5/4/4/4/5.** Changes: two-line giant headlines, a bigger tilted pouch, and the "yes." in light mint. Flaws: the pouch hangs over the split and covers the shaker; the light mint "yes." loses contrast on teal at thumbnail size; there's no fact under "Fibre: yes."
- **Final (v3): 5/5/5/5/4/4/5.** Changes: "yes." in dark teal ink (high contrast), the line "6 g fibre per pouch. Squeeze and go.", the pouch held in the top half with a mint glow, and a bigger shaker set clear of the pouch. On-brand is a 4 because the grey half is deliberately dull. That's the point, but it is less fruit-bright than the approved four.
- **Note:** grape is teal while the grape colour question is still open (decisions.md, 21 Nov). If it changes, swap the pack and the top field.

## 2. D2_AB_GrownUps.png (range: peach collagen + grape fibre): "Introducing: jelly for grown-ups."
- **Copy:** pill "Introducing" / "Jelly for" (cream) "grown-ups." (maroon) / "Fibre or collagen. Squeeze and go." / two pouches crossed / CTA "Join the waitlist".
- **Inspired by:** Bloom's "Introducing…" launch static (216 days). We keep the launch-news framing and give it an adult, warm Gooday twist that fits the brand voice ("adult, not cute-mascot"). The waitlist CTA and small print make it clear it isn't on sale yet.
- **v1: 4/4/4/4/4/4/5.** Flaws: "Introducing:" is a small, plain label that's easy to miss; only the collagen pouch shows, which doesn't fit a brand-level line; the headline is all one colour with no punch on "grown-ups."
- **v2: 5/5/5/4/4/4/5.** Changes: a maroon "Introducing" pill, "grown-ups." in maroon, a bigger headline, both lines crossed (grape fibre + peach collagen) and a soft glow. Flaws: there's no line saying what the jelly is; the pouches sit low and crowd the CTA; it reads small at thumbnail size.
- **Final (v3): 5/5/5/5/4/4/5.** Changes: the line "Fibre or collagen. Squeeze and go." sits under the headline (first attempt overlapped the pouches, so I moved it up), the pouches are resized to clear the CTA, there's a radial coral glow and a bigger headline.
- **Note:** the coral field is new (not one of the pack colours). If Justin wants to stay strictly with pack colours, swap it for the peach field.

## 3. D3_B_GymBag.png (Angle B, fibre, peach): "Gym bag, sorted."
- **Copy:** "Gym bag," (peach) "sorted." (white) / "No shaker to rinse. Squeeze and go." / chips "6 g fibre per pouch", "About 25 kcal" / CTA "Join the waitlist".
- **Inspired by:** Grüns' loud single-idea static: a solid field, huge type and the product. Here it's a dark charcoal "gym" field with peach. The moment ("in your gym bag") is Green; there are no recovery or muscle words. It's for the macro-counting gym-goer persona.
- **v1: 3/4/4/4/4/4/5.** Flaws: the headline is too small for a bold-statement ad; the pouch sits small at the bottom right with a dead middle; the flat black looks heavy and cheap.
- **v2: 4/5/5/5/4/4/5.** Changes: a 200 px headline with "sorted." in white, a big centred pouch, a peach glow and fact chips. Flaws: the centred pouch leaves the left side empty; the chips sit on the pouch's flavour name; there's no format line.
- **Final (v3): 5/5/5/5/4/5/5.** Changes: the pouch moved right and enlarged, the line "No shaker to rinse. Squeeze and go." fills the left, a warm charcoal radial background, and the chips cover only the soft-focused lower panel.
- **Note:** peach fibre is still open (peach on both lines, or collagen only). If peach becomes collagen-only, swap in the grape fibre pack on the same field.
