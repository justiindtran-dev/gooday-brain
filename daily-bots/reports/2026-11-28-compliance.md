## 5. Rules learned today (added to brain/claims.md)
1. **Stand-in in video/photos:** whenever the physical stand-in pouch is in frame, on-screen text and caption say "Stand-in pouch with our concept label" ("Concept pack shown" alone is for renders of our own shape). Never say "no konjac"/"konjac-free" or describe the gel in content that shows the konjac stand-in. Ads and finals show only our spouted shape (old shape), not the stand-in silhouette.
2. **Supply timing words:** no "on its way", "coming soon", "launching [month]" until a supplier and production date are confirmed; use "in development". "Be first" only with a waitlist that really gets first notice.
3. **kcal per SKU:** "About 25 kcal" is for the fibre pouch only (target about 26 kcal). The collagen pouch target is about 32 kcal: don't give it a kcal line until tested.
4. **Supplier emails:** every enquiry asks for a konjac-free gel; never ask a supplier for konjac-based or glucomannan jelly (11 Nov gel rule).
5. **AI and composited images (ACCC 128/26, 27 Nov):** rendered or AI-edited packs must show true size, a normal fill and no jelly, texture or gloss that hasn't been photographed from a real sample. Waitlist ads may use concept renders only with the readable "Not on sale yet. Concept pack shown…" footer. Once the format is decided, renders must match its size, net weight and closure. Before pre-orders, sales or any price in an ad, product images must be photos of the real production (or printed pre-production) pack, and "Concept pack" images come down.
