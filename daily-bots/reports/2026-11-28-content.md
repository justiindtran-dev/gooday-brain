## 0. Stand-in production rules (apply to every script and the brief)

1. **The pack is a stand-in.** It is another brand's small konjac jelly pouch (internal only, `static-ads/style-references/standin-pouch-2026-11-27/`). It is squatter than our 120 g spouted pouch, with a tear-tab top, a V-notched base and **no spout**.
2. **Printed label:** wrap the front in a print of the round 3 artwork (`gooday-launch/pouch-designs/final-with-critique/` v3, per product.md). **Print a label variant without the lower claim panel** (no "Good source of fibre" [Amber], no "Marine collagen peptides" [source open], no sugar line). If that variant isn't ready, the lower panel stays out of focus or behind real fruit in every shot. The label must cover **100% of the original brand** on the front: zoom-check the tab, seals and edges before filming.
3. **Front only.** The back still carries the other brand's print. Never turn, flip or show the back or side seals. No 360 spins.
4. **Never opened.** Don't tear the tab, squeeze, pour, bite or taste the stand-in. Its contents are konjac jelly; they never appear on camera and the pouch is never given to anyone. Justin keeps it after every shoot.
5. **No spout, no squeeze shots.** We can't show a squeeze-and-go action honestly on a tear-tab pack. Movement comes from hands placing, sliding and packing the pouch.
6. **On-screen small print in every script** from the first frame the pouch appears to the end: **"Concept pack shown. Not on sale yet. Nutrition figures are targets."** Sits on a plain area or a dark scrim, readable on a phone (claims.md rule, 27 Nov).
7. **Real fruit = flavour only.** Write "Peach flavour" / "Green grape flavour" on screen so the fruit doesn't suggest fruit content. **Never stand another fruit in for Kakadu plum** (fresh Kakadu plum is out of season; mention it in text only, no fruit shown as Kakadu plum).
8. **Collagen + gym (27 Nov rule):** the gym only as a place or a bag. No workout type, no "after legs", no sweat, stretching or muscle shots. This set uses no gym at all on the collagen script.
9. **Thai sample packs (legal.md):** tasting only, never given away. They don't appear in this set.
10. Every piece ends on **"Join the waitlist."**

## 4. UGC creator brief: "Concept pack, real fruit" ("Say it bold" at the market)

**For:** 1 to 2 Melbourne micro creators (food, fitness or wellness), 18+ audience. **Status:** draft for Justin. No creator contacted; fee or gifting is Justin's to decide (nothing offered here). Use with `daily-bots/legal/creator-gifting-agreement-draft.md` (lawyer review pending).

**What Gooday is:** a squeeze-jelly pouch in development. Fibre line: green grape (6 g fibre per pouch on the current pack). Collagen line: peach (5 g collagen per pouch, made with Kakadu plum). **Not on sale yet, and there's nothing to taste.** Goal: waitlist signups.

**The idea:** Justin meets you at a fruit shop or market (or your kitchen) with the **concept pack**: a stand-in pouch wearing a printed Gooday label. You pick the real fruit for the flavour, build a quick flat lay or hand shot, and say one bold line to camera.

**Deliverables:** 2 vertical videos, 15 to 30 s, plus raw clips.
1. **"Picked the fruit. Holding the pouch."** You choose green grapes or peaches at the stall, set them on a plain surface and place the concept pack among them (front facing camera). Hook in the first 2 s: you hold up the fruit and say the flavour ("Green grape." / "Peach."). End on the pack plus "Not on sale yet. Join the waitlist."
2. **"Say it bold."** Hold the concept pack to camera (front only, top half sharp) and deliver one line straight: **"Scoops are over."**, **"Skip the glass."** or **"Peach, sliced. Collagen, packed."** Then one honest sentence about why you'd rather not scoop powder or wash a shaker, only if it's your real experience.

**Concept pack rules (Justin runs them; you follow them):**
- It's a **stand-in, not a Gooday pouch**. Say so or keep the on-screen line **"Concept pack shown. Not on sale yet."** visible whenever it's in frame.
- **Display only.** Don't open, tear, squeeze, bite or taste it. Show the **front only**: no flipping, spinning or back shots.
- **Justin brings it and takes it home.** The concept pack and any imported sample packs stay with Gooday; nothing is given away or taken home.
- Fruit is for flavour only: say "green grape flavour" / "peach flavour", not "made with real grapes/peaches". Don't show any fruit as Kakadu plum.
- Collagen + gym: if you film near a gym, it's just a place or your bag. Don't link the collagen pouch to a workout, recovery, muscles or joints.
- Adults only in shot.

**Must include:**
- "Not on sale yet. Join the waitlist" (spoken or on screen) and the link in bio.
- Disclosure first: "Ad" or "Gifted by Gooday" in the first caption line, plus the platform's paid-partnership label (AANA Code 2.7, ACCC influencer guidance; legal.md).
- Keep the lower half of the label out of focus; don't read it out.

**You can say (Green list only):** "6 g fibre per pouch", "5 g collagen per pouch", "about 25 kcal", "under 2 g sugar", "no mixing, no shaker", "fits in your bag", "made with Kakadu plum", "less to swallow than a pile of pills", plus the bold lines above.

**Please don't say or show:** gut, bloating, digestion, skin, hair, joints, immunity, recovery, energy, weight, fullness or cravings; "guilt-free", "detox", "superfood", "prebiotic", "daily dose"; Ozempic or "GLP-1"; before and after; any taste claim about Gooday ("tastes amazing"); launch dates or "limited spots"; other brands' names or products; anything that says or implies the viewer has a health problem.
