## Top 5 picks
1. **Tombow Beverage (Japan, Toyama)**, overseas@tombow-b.jp. It is the only factory found that publishes both a **70 to 120 g spout-pouch jelly line** and a **20 g jelly-stick line**. It also holds FSSC 22000 and actively wants overseas OEM work. Cost is likely higher than in China (EST).
2. **SDFOOD (Korea, Bucheon)**, Sales@sdfoods.co.kr. It publishes **pectin or gelatin gels**, jelly-stick lines for functional foods, FSSC 22000, HACCP and K-GMP. It already makes fibre and collagen gummies.
3. **ATIQ Food (China, Nantong)**, info@atiqfoods.com. It publishes a functional line using pectin and alginate, 130 to 150 g spout pouches and squeeze sachets, BRC A, ISO 22000 and halal, with 15-day samples.
4. **Wel-Bloom (Taiwan)**, form only. It has the best certification set (ISO 22000, HACCP, NSF GMP, halal) and a dedicated functional-jelly ODM. The proprietary gel must be confirmed as not konjac.
5. **Sand-M Global (Thailand)**, export@sandm.co.th. It already makes 30 g fibre and collagen sticks, which are exactly our concepts. OEM is unconfirmed and its house gel is konjac. It is the cheapest likely route (EST) and is close to Justin.

Close runners-up: **TCI** (Taiwan; mentions TGA GMP, MOQ 200k) and **Cosmax NBT** (Korea; new 25 to 30 g jelly stick, likely a high MOQ). Both are form only.

## Concerns
- **Timing vs decisions.md:** the formula, format (pouch or stick) and collagen source are still open. The 11 Nov rule says to message manufacturers only after the ad test picks an angle. The drafts ask for quotes on both formats and both collagen sources, but Justin decides when to send.
- **ATIQ and Litafood share one office address** (Nantong Foreign Trade Center), so they are probably one group or a trading arm. Send to one first, or mention both. Ask for the factory address and audit reports.
- **Two emails were decoded from Cloudflare protection** on the official sites (Sand-M export@, Victory Foods info@). I checked each hash byte by byte, but they are not printed as plain text on the site.
- **Two addresses name a person** (tony@nawon, yasunaka@sunsho-group). Both are published on the official contact pages.
- **Some are not factories:** Sunsho Trade (an OEM coordinator) and ARNIFO (may be a sourcing company). Litafood and ATIQ list an office, not a plant.
- **Konjac is everywhere.** JoyGlo, Victory Foods and Sand-M all run konjac lines. Every draft asks for "no konjac, no glucomannan" in writing, and carrageenan only with approval.
- **Format fit:** Tombow sticks go up to 20 g only. ASEED pouches start at 150 mL. Nam Viet and Nawon have shown cups and bottles, not pouches.
- **The company email contains "sports"** (goodaysportsjelly@). That can't be avoided and it isn't a claim, but some suppliers may read it as a sports product.
- **The 22 Nov Sand-M draft is superseded.** It had placeholders ("[phone / LINE / email]") and the old 6 g / 5 g spec. Use the 23 Nov draft (#13).
- No certificate has been verified with the body that issued it. Every listed certification is as the supplier claims it.
