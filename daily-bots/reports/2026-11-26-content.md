## 0. Production rules (read first, apply to every piece)
1. **Pack:** use **round 3 only** (`gooday-launch/pouch-designs/final-with-critique/`). Live footage frames the top half of the pouch (logo, "6 g fibre per pouch" / "5 g collagen per pouch", flavour). Keep the **lower claim line out of focus** in every shot, wide shots too. That line is "Good source of fibre" (Amber) on fibre pouches and "Marine collagen peptides" (source undecided) on the collagen pouch. The outer bag's "ONE A DAY" must not be readable. Graphic end cards use the soft-focused files (`static-ads/daily/2026-11-24/source/soften.py` → `*_soft.png`).
2. **The mock-up pouch is never opened, squeezed into a mouth or tasted.** Gooday doesn't exist as food yet.
3. **Tasting rules (Scripts 1 and UGC):**
   - Category samples are **decanted into identical numbered cups**, with labels, packs and Thai text off camera or blurred. No brand, logo, pack shape or price can be identified.
   - Nobody says or shows which sample is "best", "worst", higher or lower in fibre, or how any sample compares with Gooday. **No numbers from the samples on screen.**
   - Konjac samples (the peach/melon fibre pouch, the Thai fibre stick from the factory we emailed) stay **off camera and are tasted by founders only** (ACCC konjac ban; nutritionist protocol 25 Nov). They are never given to anyone else.
   - A clear on-screen line says the cups are **not Gooday**.
   - Adults 18+ only, squeeze slowly, no children in shot.
4. Every piece ends on **"Not on sale yet. Join the waitlist."**

## 4. UGC creator brief (week of 23 Nov): "Blind jelly tasting night" + "Say it bold"

**For:** 1 or 2 AU micro creators (food, fitness or wellness), 18+ audience, based in Melbourne (the tasting happens in person). **Status:** draft for Justin. No creator contacted. Fee and gifting are Justin's to decide (not offered here). Use with the creator gifting agreement draft (`daily-bots/legal/creator-gifting-agreement-draft.md`, lawyer review pending).

**What Gooday is:** a squeeze-jelly pouch in development. The fibre line comes in grape, peach and green apple (6 g fibre per pouch on the current pack). The collagen line is peach (5 g collagen per pouch). **It's not on sale yet, and there's nothing to taste yet.** The goal is waitlist signups.

**The idea:** you join the Gooday founders' blind tasting of jellies that are already sold overseas. Then you film one bold line with a display mock-up of the Gooday pouch.

**Deliverables:** 2 vertical videos, 15 to 30 s, plus raw clips.
1. **"Blind jelly tasting night."** Numbered cups, labels hidden. Taste and react **to texture and the squeeze in your own words**: "too firm", "love the squeeze", "too sweet for me". Show your scorecard. Then end on the Gooday mock-up: "Now they're making their own. Not on sale yet."
2. **"Say it bold."** Hold the mock-up to camera (top half in frame) and deliver one line, straight and confident: **"Fibre, squeezed."** or **"5 g collagen. Zero shaker."** or **"Pick your pouch."** Follow it with one honest sentence about why you'd rather not scoop powder or wash a shaker, as long as it's your real experience.

**Tasting rules (the founders run them; you follow them):**
- You only taste cups the founders give you. These are commercially packed jellies **without konjac**, served in coded cups. Squeeze slowly. No children present.
- Don't show, say or guess brand names, pack designs, prices or the country of a sample.
- No rankings out loud or on screen ("number 1", "winner", "the best"), and no fibre or other numbers about the samples.
- Never say or imply the Gooday pouch tastes better, has more of anything, or beats any sample. The Gooday mock-up is **display only**: don't open it, squeeze it into your mouth or taste it.
- Put "Not Gooday" on screen while the cups are tasted.

**Must include:**
- "Not on sale yet. Join the waitlist" (spoken or on screen) and the link in bio.
- Disclosure first: "Ad" or "Gifted by Gooday" in the first line of the caption, plus the platform's paid-partnership label (AANA Code 2.7, ACCC influencer guidance; see legal.md).
- Pack shots on the top half only. The small lines on the bottom half of the pack are placeholders, so keep them out of focus and don't read them out.

**You can say (Green list only):** "6 g fibre per pouch", "5 g collagen per pouch", "about 25 kcal", "under 2 g sugar", "no mixing, no shaker", "fits in your bag", "squeeze and go", "made with Kakadu plum", "less to swallow than a pile of pills", plus the four bold lines above.

**Please don't say or show:** gut health, bloating, digestion, skin, hair, joints, immunity, recovery, energy, weight, fullness or cravings; "guilt-free", "detox", "superfood", "prebiotic", "daily dose"; Ozempic or "GLP-1"; before and after; any taste claim about Gooday; other brands' names or products; anything that says or implies "you" have a health problem.

**Approval:** send the edit to Gooday before posting. Gooday checks it against its claims list and replies within 2 business days.
