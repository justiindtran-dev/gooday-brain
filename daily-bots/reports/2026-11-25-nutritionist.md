## The answer in brief
1. **The Thai 10 to 13 g figures are real, but they come from a recipe Gooday can't and shouldn't copy.** Most of that fibre is **inulin/FOS** (plus psyllium, chitosan, polydextrose). The gels are often **konjac** ("บุก" is printed in Pananchita's product name). In Thailand they are registered as **dietary supplements** (ผลิตภัณฑ์เสริมอาหาร), and Doi Kham, for example, adds vitamin C at 100% Thai RDI plus vitamin B6. In Australia all three are a problem for a general-food jelly: konjac is banned, added vitamins aren't permitted, and inulin is high-FODMAP, which brings gas at 10 g.
2. **Don't move the core stick to 10 g now.** Keep **7 g** (already "excellent source", the top FSANZ tier: 10 g unlocks no new claim). Tombow, the first real factory reply (24 Nov), offers a **25 g** stick. At 25 g, 10 g of fibre would be about 44% w/w of fibre ingredient. That is too dense for a soft, squeezable, non-konjac gel.
3. **Do sample a 10 g version.** Make it resistant-dextrin-led, in a **pouch or a 35 to 40 g stick**, and ask factories to quote it as an option (Procurement). It costs only about **+A$0.03 a serve in ingredients (EST)**. Decide after the 26 Nov tasting and a small tolerance check.

## 3. Should Gooday raise fibre from 6-7 g toward 10 g?

### Feasibility in a non-konjac pectin/gellan gel
| Format | Fibre ingredient for 10 g label (design ~11 g, ~90% fibre ingredients) | % w/w | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 g stick (Tombow's offer) | ~12.2 g | **~49%** | **Not realistic.** The total solids are around 55%, so it behaves like a syrup or paste. LM pectin and gellan gels lose their squeeze and clarity, and hot-fill pumping gets hard |
| 30 g stick (current spec) | ~12.2 g | ~41% | **High risk.** Possible only with resistant dextrin (low viscosity, very soluble), and the gel may set too firm |
| 35 to 40 g stick | ~12.2 g | 30 to 35% | **Feasible to trial**. About the same density as today's 7 g / 30 g stick (28%) |
| 50 g pouch (Pananchita size) | ~12.2 g | ~24% | Feasible |
| 120 g pouch | ~12.2 g | ~10% | **Easy** |

Resistant dextrin dissolves at high levels with little viscosity, which is why it is used in fibre waters. Acacia adds viscosity, so cap it at about 2.5 g. PHGG stays at 1 g. Psyllium, oat beta-glucan and konjac are out: they swell or are banned.

### GI tolerance
- **Inulin/FOS**: gas and bloating rise with dose and are common at 10 g in one sitting. These are high-FODMAP fructans ([β-fructan meta-analysis](https://mdpi.com/2072-6643/11/1/91/htm)). This is the Thai products' main fibre, so **expect panel members to notice wind with Doi Kham and Pananchita.** That observation is useful evidence for Gooday's "gentle" story.
- **Resistant dextrin**: well tolerated. Trials ran from 8 to 30 g/day, and long-term studies to about 45 g/day ([PMC5434557](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5434557/), [Roquette tolerance](https://www.roquette.com/-/media/roquette-sharepoint-libraries/marcomonline---food-and-nutrition/roquette-food-spec-nutrition-poster-2006-helsinki-nutriose-prebiotic-effect.pdf)). A single 10 g dose gives mild symptoms in a minority of people.
- **Acacia**: tolerated to 30 g+; 10 g/day is the optimal prebiotic dose ([Calame 2008](https://www.doi.org/10.1017/S0007114508981447)). **PHGG**: about 5 to 6 g/day in IBS trials ([PMC4744437](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4744437)).
- Net: **10 g of a RD/acacia/PHGG blend is tolerable for most adults**, but more people will notice it than at 7 g, especially new fibre users and people with IBS. Pack advice stays "start with one a day, drink water". Space it from medicines by 2 hours.

### AU positioning
- FSANZ's top fibre tier is "excellent source" at **≥7 g**, so 10 g adds no claim; it only adds a bigger number. Its 10 g is 33% of the 30 g NIP reference, versus 23% at 7 g.
- In Australia, 7 g already beats the shelf: Benefiber 3 g, Metamucil and Physician's Choice gummies 5 g, Physician's Choice Fiber Jelly 6 g. The 10 to 13 g benchmark exists only in Thailand, and it uses fructans and konjac that aren't allowed here or that cause gas.
- "Gentle" fits Gooday's brand better than "most". A high dose that causes bloating would undercut a gut-comfort brand on day 3 of a subscription.

**Factory note (Tombow, 24 Nov):** Tombow's stick line can't run pectin, gellan or gelatin. It proposes **LBG + carrageenan + xanthan**, with no konjac. From a formulation view, an LBG + xanthan base with a little **iota** (soft, elastic) carrageenan can give a soft, squeezable gel. A firm **kappa** carrageenan + LBG gel would break the product rule. Carrageenan is permitted in AU and isn't the konjac ban issue, but it conflicts with the brand's "no firm carrageenan unless approved" rule and the clean-label story. **Justin decides** (open item added in decisions.md).

That makes **about 48 to 54 units**, close to the ~45 already planned (decisions.md, 24 Nov). Keep the packs and the boxes: photograph every NIP and ingredient panel for the benchmark.

**Bringing them in:** declare all food on the Incoming Passenger Card. Commercially packed, labelled food for personal use is normally allowed after inspection ([ABF/DAFF travellers](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/biosecurity-trade/travelling/to-australia)). Konjac items are for **internal tasting and factory reference only**: never give them away or sell them (ACCC konjac ban). No children on the panel. Anyone tasting must squeeze slowly.

### How to run it (about 60 min, 2 sessions)
- **Panel:** 8 to 10 adults who fit Gooday's buyer (women 25 to 45 first), plus both founders. That is directional only; a formal difference test needs 24+ people. **Exclude** anyone who is pregnant, has IBS or IBD, has a fish or shellfish allergy (Jejuvita, chitosan) or takes regular medicines.
- **Blind and coded:** decant into identical cups with 3-digit codes. Taste once from the original pack for the squeeze score only. Randomise the order. Use water and plain crackers between samples, with a maximum of 4 samples per session. Session A: fibre (Doi Kham, Pananchita, Jelly Me fibre, Posh). Session B: collagen (Jejuvita, Jelly Me collagen).
- **Score each sample (1 to 9 hedonic, unless marked):**
  1. Appearance and colour
  2. Aroma
  3. Flavour liking
  4. Sweetness (JAR: too low / just right / too high)
  5. Sourness (JAR)
  6. **Texture:** firmness (JAR), squeeze effort from the pack (1 to 5), smoothness vs grit, mouth-coating or slime
  7. **Off-notes:** bitterness, fishy or brothy (collagen), woody or medicinal, aftertaste after 1 minute (1 to 5)
  8. Overall liking
  9. "Would buy at A$2 a serve?" (definitely / probably / not)
  10. One word that describes it
- **Bench test (founder, 1 unit each):** net weight, °Brix (refractometer, EST A$30), pH (strips), squeeze-out time from the stick, syneresis (free liquid) after 24 h at room temperature and at about 30 °C. Photograph the NIP and ingredient list.
- **Optional tolerance diary (volunteers only, not medical):** on a later day, one sample of Doi Kham or Pananchita on its own. Rate gas, bloating, cramps and stool change at 4 h and 24 h (0 to 3 each). It shows what 10 to 11 g of inulin-type fibre feels like, and it backs the "gentle fibre" positioning.
- **Output:** a sheet per sample (template in the scoring list above). The Nutritionist bot will summarise the scores into formulation.md once Justin shares them.
