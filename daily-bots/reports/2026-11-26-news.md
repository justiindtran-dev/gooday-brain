## In window
1. **24 Nov, 15:28 GMT (25 Nov AM Melbourne): Supplement brands look to physician-led clinics as personalised medicine expands (NutraIngredients)**
   Tonum Health signed with Euvita Health, a physician-led longevity platform, to put its supplements into patient programs. Tonum's protein bars are pitched at GLP-1 patients who want to protect muscle.
   https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/11/24/supplement-brands-look-to-physician-led-clinics-as-personalized-medicine-expands/
   **So what for Gooday:** GLP-1 buyers are being reached through clinicians, not just ads. A dietitian channel could suit our fibre SKU later, but only with Green claims and no drug names.
2. **25 Nov: TSI Group reviews HMB evidence for muscle retention during weight loss (Nutrition Insight)**
   In a 12-week trial, a calcium HMB plus protein formula kept about 1.3 kg more muscle during diet-led weight loss. TSI admits the trial can't separate the effect of HMB from the protein.
   https://www.nutritioninsight.com/news/tsi-group-hmb-muscle-preservation-weight-loss-glp-1.html
   **So what for Gooday:** "Weight-loss companion" is crowding with protein and HMB. We stay out of the weight label (shrinking category, Red claims) and own the easy daily fibre and collagen habit.
3. **25 Nov: Nutifood (Vietnam) buys the Nourisha brand and formulas from Dairy Farmers of America, launch in Vietnam from 2027 (Nutrition Insight)**
   Nutifood's US arm takes the specialised-nutrition brand's formulas and trade marks and will co-develop products with DFA.
   https://www.nutritioninsight.com/news/nutifood-dairy-farmers-of-america-nourisha-vietnam.html
   **So what for Gooday:** Vietnamese nutrition makers are moving up the value chain. That's useful context for the Vietnam RFQs (Nawon, Sunsho; Nam Viet has no pouch line). No direct action.
4. **24 Nov: FSANZ Notification Circular 422-26: A1354, a D-psicose 3-epimerase processing aid, open for comment to 22 Dec (FSANZ)**
   This is the enzyme that makes D-psicose (allulose, a rare sugar). The circular covers only the processing aid. It doesn't approve allulose as a food ingredient.
   https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/food-standards-code/circulars/notification-circular-422-26
   **So what for Gooday:** worth watching as a future low-sugar sweetener route. Our spec stays stevia (Reb M) + monk fruit. Nutritionist bot: check allulose's AU status before anyone proposes it.
5. **24 Nov, 15:31 GMT: "Too young to age": Lumina survey of 10,500 consumers in nine markets (NutraIngredients, NI+)**
   52% want to "age slowly and healthily". Younger adults believe in supplements more (20% of 25 to 34s vs 12% of 65+).
   https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/11/24/too-young-to-age-consumers-are-rewriting-the-rules-of-longevity/
   **So what for Gooday:** this supports our 25 to 40 core buyer. Use "habit" framing, never anti-ageing or skin results (collagen Red list).
6. **24 Nov: Stack3d names Barebells and DAVID as nominees for its 2026 Functional Brand of the Year (Stack3d)**
   https://www.stack3d.com/2026/11/functional-brand-of-the-year-nominees-2026/
