## Method notes (reusable; scripts in `run-1037/source/`)
1. **Diagonal wood grain:**
   - `standin/rotfill.py` rotates the photo so the grain runs along the rows, runs `oldshape/rowfill.py`, then rotates back and composites inside the hole only.
   - Measure the grain angle with a structure tensor. Here the board joint was 27° but the grain was 23°, and the wrong angle produces chevrons.
   - Split a hole top/bottom (`left|right` strip) when one side's strip runs off the photo.
   - Finish with `jointfix.py`.
2. **Light steps inside a big fill:** `standin/lfmatch.py` re-levels the low frequencies from the wood around the hole.
3. **Palm scenes:**
   - Fill along the finger direction.
   - Restore original pixels outside the stand-in, so the real thumb stays.
   - oldshape `synth_shadow_only`: the stand-in's see-through seal tinted the warped shadow green.
   - `s_floor`: stops the stand-in's dark print reading as dirt on our white panel.
   - Segmentation misses the translucent seal and the bottom crimp, so check beside and below the pack.
4. `standin/maps.py` now falls back to a wide fill where big artwork boxes left the shading undefined.
