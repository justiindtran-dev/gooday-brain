**What makes them work (pattern list)**
1. **Short declaratives with full stops** (DAVID, Vital Proteins). Confidence comes from rhythm, not adjectives.
2. **Numbers carry the claim** (DAVID, Grüns). A checkable number beats any superlative, and it keeps the ad legal.
3. **One idea per ad, huge type, a solid colour field, the product in frame** (Grüns, Bloom pointouts).
4. **Honest bluntness or a "taboo" truth** (AG1). It works when the second line lands a plain fact, not a promise.
5. **Longevity comes from claims-light copy.** The longest runners claim the least, and that is exactly the zone Gooday's Green list allows.
