# Privacy policy

Last updated: `[date]`

This policy explains how `[Gooday company name] Pty Ltd` (ABN `[____]`) ("**Gooday**", "**we**", "**us**") handles personal information when you visit `[website]`, join our waitlist, order from us, or contact us. We aim to follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth).

## 1. What we collect
- **Waitlist and email sign-ups:** your email address, and your first name and postcode if you give them. We also note which product you were interested in and the ad or link that brought you to us.
- **Orders and pre-orders:** your name, delivery address, email, phone number, order history and subscription settings. **Card details are handled by our payment provider (Shopify Payments / `[____]`). We do not see or store your full card number.**
- **Contact and reviews:** anything you send us by email, chat or social media, and reviews or survey answers you choose to give.
- **Website and ad data:** cookies, pixels and similar tools (including the Meta Pixel and `[Google Analytics]`) collect your device and browser type, IP address, pages viewed, and actions such as sign-up or purchase. This helps us measure our ads and show our ads to people likely to be interested.
- We don't ask for health information. Please don't send it to us.

## 2. How we use it
- To run the waitlist and tell you when Gooday launches.
- To take, deliver and support your orders and subscriptions, including renewal reminders.
- To send marketing emails or SMS **only if you've agreed**. Every message has an unsubscribe link, and we act on it within 5 business days (*Spam Act 2003*).
- To measure and improve our website, products and ads (including building ad audiences on Meta from site activity or a hashed email list).
- To prevent fraud, and to meet legal duties such as tax records, consumer law and product recalls.

## 3. Who we share it with
We don't sell your personal information. We share it only as needed with:
- our online store and email providers (`[Shopify]`, `[Klaviyo / Shopify Email]`);
- our payment provider (`[Shopify Payments / Stripe / PayPal]`);
- our warehouse (3PL) and couriers (`[____]`, `[Australia Post / Sendle]`) to deliver orders;
- advertising and analytics platforms (Meta, `[Google]`, `[TikTok]`);
- professional advisers (accountant, lawyer, insurer), and regulators or authorities where the law requires, for example in a **food recall**.

## 4. Overseas
Some of these providers store or process data outside Australia, including in `[the United States, Canada, Ireland and other countries where Shopify, Meta and Google operate]`. We choose providers with reasonable security, but overseas laws may differ from Australian law.

## 5. Cookies and ad tracking
You can block or delete cookies in your browser, opt out of personalised ads in your Meta ad settings, and use `[cookie banner / "Do not sell or share" link]`. Some site features may not work without cookies.

## 6. Keeping it safe
We use reputable platforms, strong passwords and two-factor sign-in, and we limit staff access. We keep information only as long as we need it (order records are kept for 5 years for tax). If there is a data breach likely to cause serious harm, we will tell you and the OAIC where required.

## 7. Access and correction
You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or to delete your account or waitlist entry. Email us at `[privacy email, e.g. hello@____]`. We'll reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity.

## 8. Complaints
Contact us first at `[email]`. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner: [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au/) or 1300 363 992.

## 9. Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
