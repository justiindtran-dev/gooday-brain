**This agreement is made on `[date]` between:**

1. `[Gooday company name] Pty Ltd` (ACN `[____]`) of `[registered address]`, Australia ("**Gooday**"), and
2. `[Supplier legal name]` (registration no. `[____]`) of `[address, country]` ("**Supplier**").

Each is a "party". The party sharing information is the "**Discloser**" and the party receiving it is the "**Recipient**".

## 1. Purpose
The parties want to talk about the Supplier developing, sampling and possibly manufacturing jelly products for Gooday (the "**Purpose**").

## 2. What is confidential
"**Confidential Information**" means any information one party gives the other for the Purpose, in any form, whether marked confidential or not, including: formulas, recipes, ingredient lists and amounts, specifications, gel systems, samples, test results, costings and quotes, packaging artwork and designs, brand names under consideration, customer and market data, business plans, and the fact that the parties are talking.

It does **not** include information that the Recipient can show:
(a) is or becomes public without the Recipient breaking this agreement;
(b) the Recipient already lawfully knew without a duty of confidence;
(c) the Recipient receives lawfully from a third party without a duty of confidence; or
(d) the Recipient develops independently without using the Discloser's information.

## 3. What the Recipient must do
The Recipient must:
(a) use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
(b) keep it secret and protect it at least as carefully as its own confidential information, and with reasonable care;
(c) share it only with its employees, officers and advisers who need it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality duties at least as strict as these. The Recipient is responsible for them; and
(d) not reverse-engineer, analyse or copy samples or formulas, except for the Purpose.

The Recipient may disclose information if the law or a court requires it. It must first tell the Discloser (where allowed) and disclose only what is required.

## 4. Gooday's formula and designs
(a) All formulas, specifications, recipes, artwork, brand names and designs that Gooday provides, **and any formula developed specifically for Gooday from Gooday's specifications** (including improvements during sampling), belong to Gooday. The Supplier will not use them to make or sell products for anyone else.
(b) The Supplier keeps ownership of its own pre-existing know-how, processes and base recipes that it can show existed before these talks.
(c) Who owns any jointly developed formula will be set out in a later supply agreement. Until then, (a) applies.

## 5. No obligation, no licence
Neither party has to enter into a supply deal. No licence or other right is granted except the right to use the information for the Purpose. Information is given "as is".

## 6. Return or destroy
On written request, or when talks end, the Recipient must promptly return or destroy the Confidential Information (including copies), except copies it must keep by law or in automatic backups, which stay confidential.

## 7. How long
This agreement covers information shared for `[2]` years from the date above. The duty of confidence lasts **`[5]` years** after the last disclosure, and **for as long as the information remains a trade secret** in the case of formulas and recipes.

## 8. Remedies
A breach may cause harm that money can't fix. So the Discloser may seek an injunction (a court order) as well as any other remedy.

## 9. General
(a) **Law:** this agreement is governed by the laws of **Victoria, Australia**. The parties submit to the courts of Victoria. `[Option for an overseas factory: disputes go to arbitration in [Singapore] under the [SIAC] rules, in English. (b) This is the whole agreement on confidentiality. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.
(c) Neither party may transfer this agreement without the other's written consent, except Gooday may transfer it to a related company.
(d) This agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts.
(e) If a translation exists, the English version prevails.
