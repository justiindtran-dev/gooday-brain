**PREPARATION ONLY. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Legal & admin bot, 23 Nov 2026. Justin contacts the council and submits. Bots never do. Confirm the class with the council's Environmental Health team, because classes are set by each council under the *Food Act 1984* (Vic), and online and import-only models are a grey area.

## 1. Which situation are we in?
| Model | Who handles the food | Likely class (EST, confirm) | What Gooday does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. 3PL holds and ships all stock** (recommended EST) | The 3PL's warehouse | The 3PL's premises should already be registered (ask for proof, Class 3 for warehousing or distribution of pre-packaged food) | Ask **your own council** whether an importer or online seller that never holds stock must notify. Many say no registration, but notify, or a "Class 4 notification". Keep the 3PL's registration on file |
| **B. Stock at home or a storage unit; founders pack and post** | Gooday | **Class 4** if the council treats it as pre-packaged **low-risk** food (shelf-stable, no fridge). Possibly **Class 3** for "warehousing or distribution of pre-packaged foods" ([health.vic.gov.au](https://health.vic.gov.au/food-safety/food-business-classifications)) | Register (Class 3) or notify (Class 4) the council where the stock is kept. Class 3 may involve an inspection, minimum records and a fee |
| C. Market stall or sampling events | Gooday | Class 4 (pre-packaged) | Notify through **Streatrader** (the statewide system for temporary and mobile food premises) |

Why the jelly is probably "low risk" (EST): sealed, shelf-stable, hot-filled at pH ≤3.8, 12 months with no fridge ([formulation.md](../../brain/formulation.md)). Get the factory's process validation, because the council may ask.

## 2. Where to apply
- Step 1: find your council (where the stock is kept, or where the business is based) and its "Food business registration" page. Search "[council name] food business registration".
- Step 2: the Victorian [Department of Health: starting a food business](https://www.health.vic.gov.au/food-safety/starting-a-food-business-what-you-need-to-know) and its classification guidance.
- Step 3: [ABLIS (Vic): registration / notification of a fixed food premises](https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/vic/registration-notification-of-a-fixed-food-premises/24776) lists the forms and links.
- Temporary or mobile (markets): [Streatrader](https://streatrader.health.vic.gov.au/).

## 4. Documents to have ready
- [ ] Certificate of registration (ACN) and ABN
- [ ] Factory certificates (HACCP / ISO 22000 / BRC), product spec, pH and hot-fill validation, shelf-life study
- [ ] Label proof (FSANZ sign-off by the consultant)
- [ ] 3PL's council food registration (Model A)
- [ ] Recall plan and traceability (lot number → customer list)
- [ ] Product and public liability insurance certificate
- [ ] Fee payment (Justin pays, Class 3 EST A$150 to 450 a year depending on council; Class 4 usually no fee)
