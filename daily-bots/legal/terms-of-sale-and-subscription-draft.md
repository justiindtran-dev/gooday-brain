- **Consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law (ACL) can't be excluded or limited** for consumers. Never write "no refunds", "all sales final" or "we're not responsible for faulty goods". Doing so is itself misleading.

- **From 1 Jul 2027 the new ACL unfair trading practices law applies to subscriptions:** clear pre-sign-up disclosure, ongoing renewal reminders and end-of-discount reminders, and easy online cancellation ([McCullough summary](https://mccullough.com.au/2026/07/03/unfair-trading-practices-laws-australia-2027/)). - **Pre-orders:** take payment only when you're reasonably confident of a date (ACL s36). Until a factory date is confirmed, prefer the free waitlist, or a fully refundable deposit (Option B below).
