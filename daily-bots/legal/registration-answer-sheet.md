## Alternative: sole trader to start (Option B)
- Only **one** person can be a sole trader. With two founders sharing profits, the law likely treats it as a **partnership** (it needs its own ABN and a partnership tax return, and each partner is personally liable). An accountant must advise.
- ABN application: entity type "Individual / sole trader"; a business name is needed for "Gooday"; GST as above.
- Downsides for Gooday: personal liability for product claims, harder to split equity or vest shares with Kevin, and you must move everything to a company later (CGT and transfer of IP, domain, trade mark, contracts).
