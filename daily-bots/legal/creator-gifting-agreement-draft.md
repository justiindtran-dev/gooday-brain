**Date:** `[____]`

## 1. What Gooday gives
- Product: `[1 bag of 28 Gooday Fibre / Collagen sticks]`, retail value about A$`[59]`.
- `[Optional fee: A$____ paid within 14 days of the post going live, on invoice. If paid, the Creator is responsible for their own tax and GST.]`
- Gifted product is **not conditional on a positive review**. The Creator may decide not to post, or to post an honest negative opinion.

## 2. What the Creator agrees to (if they post)
- `[1]` `[Instagram Reel / TikTok / Story]` within `[30]` days of receiving the product. `[Optional, gifted: "no obligation to post".]`
- Use **their own honest opinion** and real experience only.

## 3. Disclosure (required)
Every post, story, video or caption about Gooday must make the relationship **clear at the start**, where viewers will see it without clicking "more":
- **Gifted:** "**#gifted** by @gooday" or "**Gifted** by Gooday", on screen and in the first line of the caption.
- **Paid:** "**#ad**" or "**Paid partnership with Gooday**" (use the platform's **Paid partnership / branded content label** as well).
- In video: say it out loud or show it on screen in the first seconds.
- Not enough: a tag alone, "#sp", "#collab", "#thanks", or a disclosure hidden among many hashtags.
- If the Creator later posts about Gooday again without a new deal, the disclosure still applies while there is an ongoing relationship.

## 4. What the Creator must not say (FSANZ, TGA and ACL rules)
Gooday is a **general food**, not a medicine. The Creator must not say or suggest that Gooday:
- causes **weight loss**, burns fat, speeds up metabolism or curbs appetite;
- treats, cures or prevents any disease or symptom (bloating, IBS, constipation, acne, joint pain, wrinkles);
- works like or replaces **Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro or "GLP-1"** drugs, a multivitamin or medicine;
- gives "glowing skin", "thicker hair", "faster recovery" or results in a set number of days;
- is better than a named competitor.
No before-and-after photos. No content aimed at children or teens. Show the product being **squeezed slowly** and drunk by adults.
**OK to say (Green, [claims.md](../../brain/claims.md)):** "one pouch a day", "no mixing, no shaker", "`[7]` g fibre per stick", "`[6]` g collagen per stick", "about `[21]` kcal", "I like the taste", "easy to keep in my bag", and their own honest opinion about the taste and the habit.
Gooday will send a short brief. The Creator may send a draft for a quick claims check (optional for gifting; `[required for paid posts]`). Gooday will not ask the Creator to say anything untrue.

## 5. Use of content
- The Creator owns their content.
- Gooday may **repost or share** the post on Gooday's own social accounts with credit. `[Optional: Gooday may use the content in paid ads (whitelisting / Spark Ads) for [3] months for a fee of A$____, only with the Creator's written OK.]`
- Gooday will not edit the content to change its meaning, or use it after the Creator asks it to stop (except posts already live).

## 6. Health and safety
- The Creator should read the label. People who are pregnant, on medicines, or with allergies or medical conditions should check with their doctor first. Collagen is bovine `[or marine: contains fish]`.
- If the Creator has a reaction or a concern, they should stop and tell Gooday at `[email]` (Gooday must track product safety reports).

## 7. Ending, removal
Either party can end this agreement by email. If a post breaks section 3 or 4, the Creator will fix or remove it within `[48]` hours of Gooday asking. Gooday may ask for a post to be removed if a regulator raises a concern.

## 8. General
- The Creator is independent, not an employee or agent of Gooday.
- Personal details the Creator gives (address for shipping) are handled under Gooday's [privacy policy](`[link]`) and used only to send the product.
- Governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia.
