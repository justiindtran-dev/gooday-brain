# Claims: what we can say

Planning guide, not legal advice. Copied from the Notion [Claims sheet](https://app.notion.com/p/41514ab463ca816dabdfd4a50f2a4ea3) (11 Nov 2026). Until the regulatory consultant replies, ads and pages use **Green only**. Bots check every headline against this table.

| Topic | Green: say it now | Amber: after consultant confirms | Red: never say |
|---|---|---|---|
| Format | "One pouch a day", "no mixing, no shaker", "fits in your bag", "squeeze and go" | | "Replaces your multivitamin" |
| Calories and sugar | "About 25 kcal", "under 2 g sugar", exact numbers once tested | "Low sugar" (5 g or less per 100 g) | "Zero calories", "guilt-free", "burns calories" |
| Fibre | "6 g fibre per pouch" | "Good source of fibre" (4 g+), "fibre contributes to regular laxation" | "Fixes bloating", "heals your gut", "detox", "cleanse" |
| Gut | "Made for your daily fibre habit" | "Supports a healthy digestive system" (only if a permitted claim fits) | "Better gut in 7 days", "probiotic" |
| Collagen | "5 g collagen per pouch" | "Source of protein" (5 g+) | "Glowing skin", "thicker hair", "reduces wrinkles", "repairs joints" |
| Vitamin C | "Made with Kakadu plum" | "Vitamin C contributes to normal collagen formation" (10%+ RDI per pouch) | "Boosts immunity", "prevents colds" |
| Recovery | "In your gym bag", "after training" as a moment | | "Faster recovery", "builds muscle", "reduces soreness" |
| Fullness | "6 g fibre and 25 kcal" as numbers | "Helps you feel full" (needs FSANZ notification and evidence) | "Curbs cravings", "appetite suppressant" |
| Weight | | | "Weight loss", "fat burn", "slimming", "lose X kg", "metabolism boost" |
| Drugs | | | Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, "GLP-1", "nature's Ozempic" |
| Results | | | Before and after photos, "results in 30 days" |
| Social proof | Real waitlist count, once true | | Fake reviews, invented stats, "sold out", fake timers |
| Comparisons | "Less to swallow than a pile of pills" | | "Better than [brand]", "AG1 alternative", "Grüns for Aussies" |

**Meta rules on top:** no before and after, nothing implying you know someone's body ("Struggling with bloating?"), target 18+. Write "for people who…" not "you have…".

**Also:** every health claim needs the product to pass the Nutrient Profiling Scoring Criterion. No collagen health claim exists in Schedule 4. Health claims and "daily dose" language risk making it a TGA medicine. Detail: `../daily-bots/reports/2026-11-22-research.md`.

If this page and Notion disagree, this page wins; update Notion to match.
