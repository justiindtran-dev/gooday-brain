# Customers and market

## Who buys (21 Nov 2026)
1. **Powder quitter (lead):** women 25 to 40, metro, already pay A$2 to 5 a serve for AG1, Bloom, Nutra Organics or GRNS. Hate the shaker. Fibre + greens.
2. **Fibre-curious / gut-health:** women 28 to 50, #fibregoals. Fibre + greens. Good for subscriptions.
3. **Macro-counting gym-goer:** 20 to 35, wants a 25 kcal sweet hit in the gym bag.
4. **Collagen-in-coffee woman:** 28 to 45, spends A$50+ a tub on collagen. Peach collagen.
5. **Busy parent (for themselves).**
- **Handle carefully:** GLP-1 households. Real fit, but never mention the drugs.

Full write-up and creators: `../market-research/gooday-audience-and-influencers-nov-2026.md`

## Market (hard data unless marked)
- AU domestic supplements A$5.06b in 2026 (+4.6%), A$6.96b by 2031.
- Gut A$293m (~9%/yr). Beauty/collagen A$114m (14%/yr). Weight management shrinking (−7.7%), so stay out of that label.
- Format shift: gummies and liquids gaining, powders flat. Online is 25.8% of vitamin sales.
- Australians eat ~21 to 25 g fibre a day vs 28 to 38 g targets. "Fibre is the new protein."

## Verdict
Worth pursuing as a focused online launch. Window to be first in Australia is probably 12 to 18 months (EST).
