# Product

One squeeze jelly pouch a day. Sold as a **general food** in Australia (not a TGA medicine, not a sports food). Targets for factories to quote against, not lab results.

## The two lines (as of 22 Nov 2026)

|  | Fibre + greens (Angle B, "superfood") | Collagen (Angle A) |
|---|---|---|
| Flavours | Green apple, grape (peach fibre also designed) | Peach |
| Hero | 6 g fibre (resistant dextrin or soluble corn fibre + apple pectin; acacia fibre worth asking about) | 5 g hydrolysed collagen peptides (bovine or marine, say which) |
| Extras | ~2 g greens (spinach, kale, wheatgrass); print the amount | Kakadu plum (vitamin C as whole food) |
| Energy | ~25 kcal | ~35 kcal |
| Sugar | Under 2 g; stevia or monk fruit + erythritol | Same |
| Gel | Pectin or gellan (vegan) | Gelatin or pectin |
| Pouch | 120 g spout pouch (100 to 150 g OK) | Same |
| Shelf life ask | 12 months, no fridge | Same |
| Retail idea | A$59 for 28 (A$1.80 to 2.30 a pouch), subscribe and save | Same |

Source: Notion [Product spec](https://app.notion.com/p/41514ab463ca814c8105ecc08b6c002c), updated with flavours from the final pouch designs.

## Non-negotiables
- **No konjac, no glucomannan.** ACCC permanently bans konjac mini jelly cups. No firm-setting carrageenan blends unless approved. Soft enough to drink through the spout.
- Pack warning: "Squeeze slowly. Not suitable for children under 5."
- **No added vitamins** until the regulatory consultant answers. Jelly isn't a permitted food for added vitamins under Std 1.3.2 / Schedule 17.
- Marine collagen means a **fish allergen** declaration.

## Open product questions
- Format: 120 g spout pouch, or a 15 to 30 g tear-and-squeeze stick (cheaper, what Physician's Choice and Sand-M use). See [decisions.md](decisions.md).
- Collagen source (bovine or marine), and halal or not.
- Peach on both lines, or peach for collagen only (design critique suggests the latter).
- Bag size: 14 or 28 pouches.
