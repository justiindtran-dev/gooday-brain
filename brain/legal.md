# Legal and admin: paperwork register

Legal & admin bot, first version 23 Nov 2026. **Planning guide, not legal, tax or accounting advice.** Items marked "lawyer" or "accountant" need a professional before anyone signs or files. Bots never file, register, pay, sign or contact anyone. Justin (or Kevin, a lawyer or a broker) does that.

- **Costs:** government fees are linked where checked (23 Nov 2026). Everything else is **EST** (no quote yet).
- **Links:** the official pages were opened on 23 Nov 2026 only where a source is cited in the text. Other links point to the right agency but may have moved, so search the agency site if one breaks.
- **Status:** nobody has filed anything yet. Every item starts at **Not started**. **Unknown** means a founder may have done it already (for example, holds an ABN personally). Founders: please correct.
- **Drafts and pre-filled answer sheets:** `../daily-bots/legal/` (see the list at the bottom).
- Supplier country (Thailand, Taiwan, China or an AU co-packer) is still open ([decisions.md](decisions.md)). Import items assume Asia. If NHP (South Australia) wins, skip the import rows.

## The first five (do these in order)
1. **Decide the structure** (open in [decisions.md](decisions.md)): a Pty Ltd owned by both founders (recommended EST, because there are two founders and a product-liability risk) or a sole trader to start. Talk to an accountant first (EST A$200 to 500).
2. **Director IDs** for Justin and Kevin. Free, and each person applies for their own. You need them before the company is registered.
3. **Register the Pty Ltd and get the ABN** (company A$636 from 1 Jul 2026). Then register the business name "Gooday" if it differs from the company name (EST A$45 a year), and the domain.
4. **Co-founder agreement + IP assignment**, signed before money comes in or a supplier gets the formula. Draft term sheet in `../daily-bots/legal/`. A lawyer turns it into a shareholders agreement (EST A$1.5k to 4k).
5. **Trade mark TM Headstart** for Gooday in classes 5 and 29 (A$200 a class), before you buy a domain or print packs ([brand.md](brand.md)).

- **Sample imports:** "Commercial samples are treated in the same manner as commercial quantities" ([Agriculture FAQ](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/biosecurity-trade/import/goods/biological/faq)). Check BICON for collagen/gelatin jelly **before** samples ship; permits take about 20 working days after a complete paid application (same source). Each parcel: commercial invoice with a realistic value, ingredient list with %, collagen source declaration, CoA. Keep under A$1,000 per consignment (EST).
- **Supplier documents to ask for at the sample stage:** certificates with number, issuer and expiry (verify with the issuer); spec sheet; batch CoA (micro, heavy metals, fibre/protein with method); allergen statement for product and line against the AU list (peanut, tree nuts, egg, milk, sesame, soy, fish, crustacea, mollusc, wheat, gluten, lupin, sulphites ≥10 mg/kg; [FSANZ PEAL](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/food-allergies/allergen-labelling)); written no konjac/glucomannan/carrageenan incl. line cross-contact; collagen/gelatin species, tissue (hide vs bone), country, BSE/TSE and halal; ingredient origins and FTA certificate-of-origin ability; factory address and latest audit report; product liability insurance certificate.
