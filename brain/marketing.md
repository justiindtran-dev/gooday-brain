- **Pack realism method (27 Nov, from Justin's stand-in photos `static-ads/style-references/standin-pouch-2026-11-27/`):** re-skin a real photographed pouch instead of rendering one: cut it out, take its low-frequency shading (multiply into the Gooday art at 1.4 to 1.6x) and its long thin highlight streaks (screen over the art; dots dropped so no old print survives), keep the real seal edges and shadow, cap printed white at the photo's own white-print level, and cover the soft-focused claim panel with real fruit. Render-only cues (halftone, misregistration, scuffs, crinkle noise) barely show at ad size and hurt when overdone.
